How to Choose the Right Travel Itinerary

A good travel itinerary helps you make the most of your time while leaving enough flexibility to enjoy the destination. Before choosing a trip plan, consider how many days you have, the places you want to visit, your travel style and the amount of time needed for transportation.

Short City Break Itineraries

Short itineraries are useful for travelers planning a weekend getaway or a quick city break. A three- or four-day itinerary can focus on major attractions, neighborhoods, local food and a few memorable experiences.

Multi-Day International Itineraries

Longer trips allow travelers to explore several destinations at a comfortable pace. Our travel itinerary ideas include European trips, Japan, Thailand and India, helping international travelers understand how destinations can be combined into one journey.
