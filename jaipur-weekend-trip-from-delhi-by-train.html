Jaipur Weekend Trip from Delhi by Train: 2-Day Plan with Timings

Written by Laxmi Pal | Last updated: October 2026

Quick Answer

Yes, you can cover Jaipur's main sights in a weekend trip from Delhi. Take a morning train from Delhi to Jaipur (about 4.5 to 5.5 hours), spend Day 1 in the old city (City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Hawa Mahal, bazaars), spend Day 2 at Amber Fort and Albert Hall Museum, and return by an evening train. A 2-day composite ticket covers most of these monuments.

Key Takeaways
  • Distance: Delhi to Jaipur is about 270 km by road and roughly 290 to 300 km by rail.
  • Travel time by train: About 4.5 to 5.5 hours, depending on the train.
  • Best time to visit: October to March, when the weather is pleasant.
  • Days needed: 2 days is enough for the main highlights.
  • Best ticket option: The Jaipur composite ticket is valid for 2 consecutive days and covers Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar, Albert Hall Museum and Nahargarh Fort. It does not cover City Palace.
  • Best Day 2 start: Amber Fort in the early morning, before the crowds.

My Experience

I visited Jaipur with my friends, and it was a really enjoyable trip. We spent our time exploring the forts and palaces, walking through the old city bazaars and trying local food.

We enjoyed exploring the city together, and it was one of those trips that is even more fun with friends.

If you are planning a trip with friends, a weekend is enough to enjoy the best of the Pink City without feeling rushed.

How to Reach Jaipur from Delhi by Train

Short answer: Several fast trains run between Delhi and Jaipur every day, and the journey takes about 4.5 to 5.5 hours. Book on the IRCTC website and check live timings on the Indian Railways NTES portal, because schedules and fares change.

Trains leave from stations such as New Delhi, Delhi Cantt or Old Delhi (check your train's station) and arrive at Jaipur Junction. Options include the Delhi Cantt to Ajmer Vande Bharat Express, which stops at Jaipur, the New Delhi to Ajmer Shatabdi Express, and regular express trains, which are usually slower but may be cheaper.

Tips for booking:

  • Book early for weekends, because seats fill up fast, especially from October to March.
  • Pick a morning departure on Saturday so you get a full first day.
  • Pick an evening return on Sunday, but leave a buffer of at least 1 hour before the train.

Jaipur Composite Ticket: Save Money on Monuments

Short answer: The Jaipur composite ticket gives single entry to several government-run monuments and is valid for 2 consecutive days from the date of purchase, which fits a weekend trip perfectly.

It covers Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar, Albert Hall Museum, Nahargarh Fort and a few gardens. It does not cover City Palace, and it is not valid for the evening light show at Amber Fort, which has its own ticket. Prices differ for Indian visitors, students and foreign tourists, and they can change, so confirm the current rate at the ticket counter or on the official Rajasthan Department of Archaeology and Museums website.

Day 1: Old City Sightseeing

9:00 AM to 11:00 AM: Arrive and Check In

Reach Jaipur Junction, take a cab or auto to your hotel, and freshen up. If your room is not ready, ask the hotel to hold your bags and start with breakfast nearby.

12:00 PM to 2:00 PM: Lunch

Try a Rajasthani thali or dal baati churma at a local restaurant. This is a good time to rest, because the afternoon sun can be strong.

2:30 PM to 5:00 PM: City Palace and Jantar Mantar

Visit the City Palace, where part of the complex is still linked to the royal family and the museum shows costumes, weapons and royal items. Buy its ticket separately, since it is not part of the composite ticket.

Right next to it is Jantar Mantar, an astronomical observatory built by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II between 1728 and 1734. It has around 20 large stone instruments and was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2010. A guide makes this visit much more interesting.

5:30 PM to 7:30 PM: Hawa Mahal and Local Bazaars

Photograph Hawa Mahal, the "Palace of Winds", in the late afternoon light. Then walk through Johari Bazaar and Bapu Bazaar for block-print fabrics, jewellery, mojari footwear and handicrafts. Bargain politely.

8:00 PM: Dinner

Try pyaaz kachori, laal maas (for non-vegetarians) or a thali. End the day with a lassi or kulfi.

Day 2: Amber Fort and Return

8:00 AM to 11:00 AM: Amber Fort

Amber Fort (also written as Amer Fort) opens in the morning and is open until the evening for daytime visits, so reach early to avoid crowds and heat. It is about 11 km from the city centre, and you should set aside 2 to 3 hours. Walk through the courtyards, mirror halls and gateways, and enjoy the views over Maota Lake. Wear comfortable shoes.

11:15 AM: Jal Mahal Photo Stop

On the way back, stop at the roadside viewpoint for Jal Mahal, the palace that seems to float on Man Sagar Lake. You usually cannot go inside, but it is a good quick stop.

12:30 PM to 3:30 PM: Lunch and Albert Hall Museum

Have lunch, then visit Albert Hall Museum, one of Jaipur's most beautiful buildings, with a collection of art, textiles and artefacts.

4:00 PM onwards: Pick Up Bags and Head to the Station

Collect your luggage, reach Jaipur Junction at least 1 hour before your train, and enjoy the evening ride back to Delhi.

How Much Does a Jaipur Weekend Trip from Delhi Cost?

Short answer: The cost depends on your train class, hotel and food choices, so check current prices before booking. The main expenses are:

  • Train tickets (both ways)
  • Hotel for 1 night
  • Local transport (auto, cab or app-based rides)
  • Monument entry (the composite ticket plus a separate City Palace ticket)
  • Food and shopping

Budget travellers can save by choosing a simple hotel, taking a non-AC or sleeper train, eating at local restaurants and sharing cabs with friends.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hours does it take to go from Delhi to Jaipur by train?

Most trains take about 4.5 to 5.5 hours. Faster services such as the Vande Bharat Express and Shatabdi are usually at the lower end of that range.

Is 2 days enough for Jaipur?

Yes. Two days are enough to see Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Hawa Mahal and the main markets. A third day lets you add Nahargarh Fort at sunset and a slower pace.

What is the best time for a Jaipur weekend trip from Delhi?

October to March is the most comfortable time. Summers (April to June) can be very hot, so start sightseeing early in the day.

Is the Jaipur composite ticket worth it?

Yes, if you plan to visit several monuments. It is valid for 2 consecutive days and covers Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar, Albert Hall and Nahargarh Fort, but not City Palace.

Is train better than car for Delhi to Jaipur?

Trains avoid highway traffic and are usually cheaper, while a car gives you flexibility and door-to-door travel. For a short weekend trip, a morning train is a stress-free option.

Which places should I not miss in Jaipur in 2 days?

Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar and Hawa Mahal are the top choices for first-time visitors.

Practical Tips

  • Check train timings and monument opening hours on official websites before you go.
  • Start early each day, because the best light and the smallest crowds are in the morning.
  • Wear light cotton clothes and comfortable shoes, and carry water and sunscreen.
  • Fix the fare before you board an auto-rickshaw, or use a cab app.
  • Cover your shoulders and knees at religious places, and ask before photographing people.
